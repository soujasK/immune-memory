## Judging and track fit

The exact dimensions are Impact Potential & Innovation, Execution Quality, and Presentation & Clarity. High scores require original value beyond routine replication, rigorous validation supporting conclusions, and readily understood reporting. Exceptional scores emphasize a new direction or approach, unusually robust execution, and exemplary communication. We use this to identify shortcomings, without claiming a numerical score.

Track 1 covers reproducible sandboxes, shared resources and containment. Track 2 concerns network behavior, topology and propagation, with examples involving malicious spread and shared refusals. Signal ≠ Safety fits Track 2 through paired direct, one-intermediary and three-intermediary communication. Track 3 concerns identity, reputation and commitments, including renaming and protocol attacks. Track 4 concerns oversight of collective behavior, distributed harm and partial visibility. Comparing a message-only predictor against an independently scored action fits Track 4. A distinction between text fidelity and action compliance is essential to both tracks.
